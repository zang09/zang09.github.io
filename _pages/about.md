I am an Integrated Ph.D. student in the Interdisciplinary Program in Artificial Intelligence at [Seoul National University](https://gsai.snu.ac.kr/), advised by [Jaesik Park](https://jaesik.info/). My research interests include sensor calibration, novel view synthesis, and 3D reconstruction.

Before starting my Ph.D., I completed my military service and worked as a robotics software engineer at [Kakao Mobility](https://www.kakaomobility.com/) and Stryx, focusing on LiDAR-based localization, SLAM, and robot navigation.

I received my B.S. degree in Robotics from Kwangwoon University. During my undergraduate years, I was awarded a full scholarship through my active involvement in the university’s robotics team, [RO:BIT](https://www.youtube.com/c/ROBITKORrobotgameteam/), where I served as the team leader of the Humanoid division.
