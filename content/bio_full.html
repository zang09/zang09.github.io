I worked at Kakao Mobility (2021–2022) as a robotics software engineer. I built the data-collection software for the self-driving fleet and worked on visual SLAM and localization. Before that I completed my military service at Stryx (2019–2021) as a lead software engineer, where I developed sensor-capture software for mobile mapping systems and an autonomous driving stack for unmanned ground vehicles covering SLAM, localization, sensor fusion, and path planning. I also interned at ROBOTIS in 2019 and tested the Open Manipulator before its open-source release.

I received my B.S. in Robotics from Kwangwoon University. I was a member of the humanoid division of RO:BIT, the university's robot game team, for three years and led it in 2018. We won awards at RoboCup and national competitions, and I received a full scholarship for it.

I serve as a reviewer for NeurIPS, ECCV, CVPR, and IEEE TIP.
