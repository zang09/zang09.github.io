I am an Integrated Ph.D. student in the Graduate School in AI at Seoul National University, where I work in the SNU VGI Lab advised by Jaesik Park. My research lies at the intersection of robotics and 3D reconstruction: building sensing pipelines and scene representations that let robots perceive and reconstruct the world, from LiDAR–camera calibration to neural rendering with 3D Gaussian Splatting. Before graduate school I spent several years as a robotics software engineer working on SLAM, localization, and autonomous navigation.
